# Rule baseline and held-out evaluation

What M7 is compared against, on which data, and what came out. Code:
[`app/lab/rule_baseline.py`](../../backend/app/lab/rule_baseline.py) and
[`app/lab/heldout.py`](../../backend/app/lab/heldout.py). `make train` writes the deliverables to
[`ml/reports/`](../../ml/reports):

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `fin04_eval.json` | Every number below, with 95% intervals, per-typology recall, look-alikes by type, feature importance, the live-feed case |
| `heldout_predictions.csv` | One row per case per test set: label, RB-1 clauses, RB-2 priority, M7 score and alert, top features, supporting record IDs |
| `rule_baseline.json` | RB-1 and RB-2 definitions and their results |
| `model_card_m7.json` | M7's model card |

The Twin Lab page shows the same report (*M7 against the documented rule baselines*), served by
`GET /api/v1/lab/model-eval`.

**Every number is measured on synthetic Kestrel-Sim worlds.** None is a claim about real-bank
accuracy. Synthetic schemes separate more cleanly than real ones, so absolute values are high. The
comparisons between rows, and the places where rules beat the model, are what carry over.

## The baselines

### RB-1 — documented rule set (v1.0)

A case alerts when **any** clause holds. Each clause is built from SUTRA's own detectors and has no
learned parameter. The clauses were written from the detector catalogue before any evaluation ran, and
they are frozen: a change means RB-1.1 and a new row, never a silent edit.

| Clause | Typology | Fires when |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Account takeover | R4: a mobile, password or e-mail change, then a new payee paid within 24 h |
| C2 | Insider misuse | R7 override use and R6 access with no purpose on the same account, or R9 an entitlement used after its intended expiry |
| C3 | Mule network | G4 mule factory, or G2 rapid pass-through and R10 presence-less opening on the same account |
| C4 | Structuring | R1 cash structuring spread across at least 2 branches |
| C5 | Layering | G1 time-respecting cycle returning 70–110% of the first hop |
| C6 | Dormant takeover | R3 debit from a dormant account and R2 limit-hugging transfers from the same account |

### RB-2 — SUTRA's lattice, rules only

A case alerts when any of its chains reaches P1, P2 or P3 on the [priority lattice](../../README.md)
with every model input off (`compute(models=False)`: no M5, no M6). It measures the hand-written argument
rules on their own.

### The models

| Row | What it is |
|---|---|
| **M7** | LightGBM over all 32 [case features](features.md); Platt calibration on grouped out-of-fold scores; alert at the threshold that maximises out-of-fold F1 |
| **M7 without detector features** | The same over the 25 graph and sequence features (ablation) |

## The test sets

| Set | Data | Models used |
|---|---|---|
| **Unseen connected cases** | All cases in training worlds 7–10, scored out-of-fold (`StratifiedGroupKFold`, 5 folds, grouped by ring) | Fold models |
| **Held-out worlds** | Worlds 11 (randomised) and 42 (the demo story) — never used for fitting, thresholds or feature choices | Final models, threshold frozen |
| **Later events** | Held-out cases (worlds 11 and 42 + the 22 Sep feed) with any event on or after 2 Sep | Models refitted only on training cases that **ended before** 2 Sep |

Look-alikes are in every set. Intervals come from 1,000 bootstrap resamples **of groups**, not of cases,
so a ring that spans several cases is resampled whole.

## What the numbers say

- **Where M7 adds most: look-alikes and unlinked mule fragments.** RB-1 alerts on 82 of 83 pension-camp
  twins and all 12 monthly loan loops in the training worlds, because R4 and G1 fire on them as they are
  meant to. M7 alerts on none. Mule-ring fragments that never connect to a takeover are caught by RB-1
  about half the time (0.52 out-of-fold, 0.63 held-out), never by RB-2 (no chain, so nothing to argue),
  and by M7 in every case.
- **Where rules still win.**
  - *Circular flows.* RB-1 and RB-2 catch every circular flow. M7 misses 1 of 12 out-of-fold and 1 of 4
    held-out. The held-out miss is the demo world's 8 Sep loop, which stays **P2** in the queue because
    M7 may only reorder alerts within a band, never demote one.
  - *Later events, ranking.* RB-2 has a higher PR-AUC (0.92 against 0.84).
  - *Later events, loan loops.* M7 trained on the past alerts on all 4 monthly loan loops in that set,
    just as RB-1 does. The cause is the training data. A monthly loop recurs, so none of the 12 in the
    training worlds had ended by the cutoff (all run to 4 Sep), while 10 of the 12 suspicious circular
    flows had. The past-only model learned "a cycle means fraud" without a single legitimate
    counterexample. This is temporal shift, and the full model, which saw the loops, alerts on none of
    them.
- **Later events are the honest test.** With models refitted on cases that ended before 2 Sep, M7's
  precision falls from 1.00 to 0.84 and its look-alike false-positive rate rises from 0.00 to 0.09.
  That is still about half of RB-1's rate on the same set (0.19).
- **Structure carries the signal.** Without any detector feature, M7 keeps 0.91 precision and 0.88
  recall on held-out worlds, against RB-1's 0.42 and 0.91.
- **At the same alert budget.** Given RB-1's 71 alerts on held-out worlds, M7's top 71 cases contain all
  33 suspicious cases, where RB-1 finds 30.

## How M7 reaches the queue

M7 never sets a priority level. The lattice still decides the band (P1, P2, P3 or Watch) from argued
evidence, and M7 orders alerts **inside** each band. Each alert carries its connected case
(`argument.case`): the case key, linked cases, detectors, and M7's suspicion score with its top
contributions and supporting records. The score is shown between 0.01 and 0.99 and labelled
*"Suspicion score (model) — a reason to look first, not a finding"*. Determination stays with
four-eyes review. Without the artifact, the queue falls back to rules-only ordering.

## Reproduce

```bash
make train
```

Takes about 80 s on a laptop: seven worlds on four processes, then training and evaluation. Then:

```bash
uv run --no-sync pytest backend/tests/test_m7.py
```
